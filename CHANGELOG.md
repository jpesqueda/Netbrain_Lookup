# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Standalone packaging of the 2.6.4 RC4 development engine.
- CLI and Textual interactive mode, collapsible summary, filterable results and row details.
- Configurable direct lookup workers, fast/standard/deep flags, CA bundle and non-interactive mode.
- Serialized session renewal, thread-local request errors, no renewal during logout.
- Input/output collision protection, atomic CSV and basic spreadsheet formula protection.
- Domain selection required; OneIPList shape validation; final error status corrected.
- Bilingual usage docs, CI, manual TestPyPI and PyPI workflows.
- Distinct direct observations, per-target summary counts and exact IPv4 matching.
